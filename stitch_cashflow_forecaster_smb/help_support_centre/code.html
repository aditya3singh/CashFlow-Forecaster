Bank Connections

Connecting a new bank expand_more
Go to Settings > Integrations and select "Add Institution" to securely link your accounts via Plaid.
Troubleshooting sync issues expand_more
If your account isn't updating, try forcing a manual refresh from the Connections menu. If issues persist, re-authenticate.

Account Security

Enabling 2FA expand_more
Protect your account by enabling Two-Factor Authentication in Settings > Security. We support SMS and Authenticator apps.
Updating your password expand_more
You can change your password anytime from your profile settings. Ensure it meets our minimum strength requirements.
How we encrypt your data expand_more
All personal and financial data is encrypted at rest using AES-256 and in transit via TLS 1.3 protocols.
